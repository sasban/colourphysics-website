Two people can look at the same sample and disagree.

Light, surroundings, eyesight and fatigue all change what we see. Words like “bright”, “coppery” or “cornflower” can’t be checked or compared — so quality disputes turn into opinions.

Measurement settles it. A controlled light, a calibrated instrument and an agreed standard turn appearance into numbers that a buyer and seller can both accept.

What a report shows

Numbers first. Then what they mean.

Each report compares your sample with its standard, flags anything outside tolerance, and says in plain words what changed and what to look at. It is signed off by a colour scientist before it reaches you.

  • Colour differenceΔE*, with lightness, chroma and hue
  • Colour coordinatesCIE L*a*b* and L*C*h
  • Spectrumreflectance / transmission, 400–700 nm
  • Appearanceparticle size, fibre %, uniformity
  • Tolerance checkpass / review / fail, against your limits
  • Trendbatch history in your portal

Instruments

Huey™ and the spectrometer

Huey is our imaging system: a calibrated camera and controlled lighting in a closed cabinet, with machine-vision software that measures colour, size and shape across a whole sample — not just one spot.

For gemstones and transparent samples, a spectrometer records the full spectrum, so colour can be described and compared exactly.
